# Google Play store listing

Copy these into Play Console → *Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing*.
The default language is English (United States), shown in every country without its own translation; add Romanian as a translation (*Manage translations → Romanian*), which Romanian phones see automatically.
Character limits are Google's: app name 30, short description 80, full description 4000.

```
Clever Owl turns first lessons into play! Children learn letters, numbers, first words and simple math with Hupi, a curious little owl, through stories and 24 colourful games, all spoken aloud.

🦉 HUPI'S ADVENTURE – a story mode in 7 chapters. The naughty magpie took the forest's colours, numbers and letters, and your child helps Hupi get them back. 🔢 NUMBERS – number cards from 1 to 20, counting with a finger ("one, two, three…"), find the number and number balloons

📖 READING – first words: read the word and find its picture, or listen and find the word

✏️ WRITING – trace capital letters, small letters and numbers with a finger, with arrows showing the stroke order, then spell words letter by letter

➕ MATH – counting, adding and taking away with pictures, "Which side has more?"

🎨 COLOURS & SHAPES – colours, shapes and a free drawing studio with a rainbow crayon, stamps and saving to the gallery

🐾 ANIMALS – 28 farm and wild animals with real sounds: tap the cow and hear "Moo!"

⭐ THREE LEVELS – Easy, Medium and Hard. Set your child's age and the level follows; change it at any time.

🏆 REWARDS – stars, a 40-sticker album, sticker scenes to decorate, a daily gift and Hupi's house, with shelves where everything your child learned shines

🗣️ NO READING NEEDED – everything is spoken, so even pre-readers can play on their own

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR PARENTS – a grown-ups' corner behind a parental gate: your child's age and name, a daily play-time limit, a learning diary (what's learned and what's still being practised), voice choice

🔒 NO ADS. NO SUBSCRIPTIONS. NO DATA COLLECTION.
```
