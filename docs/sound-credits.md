# Animal sound credits

The animal sounds in the Animals world are real recordings, used under free licences.
Each file in `app/src/main/res/raw/animal_*` was shortened to its first few seconds (Ogg pages
cut without re-encoding). The same list is shown in the app: grown-ups' corner → Open-source
licences.

Every sound lasts at least 2 seconds (`MinSoundMs` in `AnimalSoundPlayer`): shorter recordings
(goat, pig, elephant, parrot) are repeated with a short pause, and the sheep plays three different
bleats.

CC BY recordings (monkey, bear) require this credit; it is shown in the app. CC0 and public
domain recordings need no credit, but they are listed here too.
